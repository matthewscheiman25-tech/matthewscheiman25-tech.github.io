What ELARVIS collects

ELARVIS does not collect, store, or share your personal information.

  • The game has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or files.
  • The game does not contain advertising in this version.
  • The game does not use third-party analytics trackers in this version.

Game progress and settings are stored on your device only.

Purchases

If you buy anything in ELARVIS, the payment is processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment information under Google's own privacy policy. ELARVIS never sees or stores your card number or payment details.

Children

ELARVIS is intended for an adult audience (18+). It is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version of ELARVIS adds accounts, online features, ads, or analytics, this policy will be updated first and the update will be posted at this same address.
